## Scope

**Variants:** `default` (bare radio list), `boxed` (bordered row cards), `card` (visual tile picker)

**States:** resting-unselected, hover-unselected, resting-selected, hover-selected, active-selected, focus-visible, disabled (exempt)

**Sizes:** `small`, `medium`

**Composition:** `legend`, `helperText`, `errorMessage`, `description` per option, `disabled` per option, `required`

**Surfaces:** `default`, `paper`, `elevated` in both `light` and `dark` mode

**Out of scope:** reversed / brand-surface placement (no reversed RadioGroup variant exists in the design system). Custom `color` prop values other than `primary`.
