# Eligibility Checker

A compact, config-driven eligibility pre-gate. It is a **feature** — it owns its own step navigation, conditional follow-ups, and outcome logic. See [Components vs Features](/?path=/docs/foundation-components-vs-features--docs) and the [feature-doc template](/?path=/docs/features-_template--docs).

---

## 1. What & when

Use it to gate a journey behind a few yes/no questions before the member commits. One question per card, conditional follow-ups, and an outcome: a success callout, an advisory warning the member can pass, or a hard "no" that disables Next.

---

## 2. Public API

| Prop | Type | Default | What it changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `config` | `EligibilityCheckerConfig` | — | The whole question set, branches, and outcome wording. **This is the variation surface.** |
| `defaultStep` | `number` | first step | Seed the starting step (resume/demo). |
| `defaultAnswers` | `Answers` | `{}` | Seed answers (resume/demo). |
| `defaultEligible` | `boolean` | `false` | Start in the eligible (success) state. |

---

## 3. Variants

The variation is the **config**, not the component. Two configs exist today.

| Variant | Config | UX difference |
|---|---|---|
| Lifetime Pension | `lifetimePensionConfig` | Minimum-transfer, tax-deduction, and retirement-status questions. |
| Retirement Income Account | `retirementIncomeAccountConfig` | Tax-deduction branch then retirement status. |

## 4. Data contract

- **Input** — a single `config` plus optional seed props.
- **Output** — the member’s `Answers` and an eligible/ineligible outcome, surfaced inline; the host reads the result to decide whether to continue.

---

## 5. Constraints

- Conditional follow-up questions appear based on prior answers.
- A hard "no" disables Next; an advisory warning leaves it enabled.

---

## 6. Composition / where-used

> **Hand-maintained** until features are registered in the codemap (follow-up). Drives the eligibility step in the Lifetime Pension and Retirement Income Account applications.
