## Scope

- Variants: exclusive (radio-group behaviour), multi-select
- Sizes: `small`, `medium`, `large`
- States: unselected resting, unselected hover, selected resting, selected hover, selected active, focus-visible, disabled
- Label and error-message composition (`label`, `error`, `errorMessage` props)
- Surfaces: `default`, `paper`, `elevated` in both light and dark mode

Out of scope: reversed/brand-surface use (no reversed ToggleButton variant exists in the design system).
